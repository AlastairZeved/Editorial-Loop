# Schema: Compression Pass

## Recognition Trigger
Text has been written or rewritten. Words exist on the page. Now evaluate whether each one earns its place.

## Execution Sequence

**WEIGHT TEST — sentence by sentence:**

For each sentence in the passage:
1. Read the sentence aloud (or simulate reading aloud — hear the rhythm).
2. Remove one word at a time. After each removal, ask:
   - Does the meaning change? If no → the word is dead weight. Delete it.
   - Does the meaning hold but the FEELING change? If the sentence becomes technically correct but emotionally flat → the word is weight-bearing. Keep it.
3. After all dead weight is removed, read the compressed sentence in context (sentence before, compressed sentence, sentence after).

**FLUFF SCAN:**
- Qualifiers that soften but don't clarify ("somewhat," "rather," "quite," "a bit")
- Hedges that protect the author instead of serving the reader ("arguably," "it could be said that," "one might suggest")
- Redundant pairs ("each and every," "first and foremost," "completely and totally")
- Throat-clearing openings ("It is important to note that," "It should be mentioned that," "The fact that")

If any found → delete. Rewrite the sentence without them. Re-read in context.

**EXPANSION CHECK:**
After compression, re-read the full passage. Ask:
- Did I compress something that needed to breathe? A moment of recognition, a beat where the reader sits with an idea, an emotional turn?
- Did I strip a sentence that was doing two jobs — carrying meaning AND carrying feeling — and leave only the meaning?
- If yes → expand that specific point. But expand only with weight-bearing words. Not filler. Not qualifiers. Words that do work.

**RHYTHM CHECK:**
Read the compressed passage for rhythm. Compression that destroys rhythm has gone too far. Short sentences need long ones around them. Dense passages need a breath. If the passage reads like a telegram → expand strategically for rhythm, not for content.

## Scale
- **Sentence level:** Primary scale. Weight test and fluff scan operate word-by-word within sentences.
- **Paragraph level:** Expansion check and rhythm check operate across sentences within a paragraph.
- **Section level:** Check rhythm variation across paragraphs within a section (all short paragraphs = telegraphic, all long = dense)
- **Document level:** Not primary — defer to Ripple Read for document-scale rhythm.

## Completion Test
Every word in the passage is doing work — carrying meaning, carrying feeling, or carrying rhythm. No word exists because it was there before and wasn't removed. No gap exists where a weight-bearing word was stripped.

## Measurable Features
- Dead-weight words remaining (qualifiers, hedges, redundancies, throat-clearing): target 0
- Sentences where removal of any word changes meaning or feeling: target 100%
- Rhythm variation present (mix of sentence lengths): yes/no
- Emotional weight markers preserved: count of (a) moments of recognition, (b) reflective beats/pauses, (c) emotional turns — pre-compression vs. post-compression. Target: no net loss.
